\subsection*{Statements}

\paragraph*{Use of generative AI}
Generative AI was used in preparing this paper and is recorded here as part of the
method. Claude Opus 5 (Anthropic), used through Claude Code, assisted with the
implementation of the contracts, the issuance service and the measurement scripts,
and produced first drafts of the running text, which the author revised. The
questions, the scope, the design decisions and the judgements of what to claim and
what to leave open are the author's. Every measurement reported here is
reproducible from the accompanying repository, and every citation was checked
against its source. No generative model is credited as an author, and the author
is responsible for the entire content.

\paragraph*{Data and code availability}
The contracts, test suite, issuance service, measurement scripts and the data
files behind every figure and table in this paper are available at
\url{https://github.com/TakatoPhy/animator-sbt-paper} under the MIT licence. The
gas figures are regenerated by \texttt{scripts/measure\_gas\_local.js} and the cost
model by \texttt{paper/figures/cost\_simulation.py}.

\paragraph*{Conflict of interest}
The author is the founder of TOONIQ LLC, which develops production-management
software for the animation industry and would be a party to any deployment of the
framework described here.
