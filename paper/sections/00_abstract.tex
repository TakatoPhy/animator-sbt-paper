Japan's anime industry, valued at over \$34 billion globally, relies heavily on freelance animators who constitute 47--70\% of the production workforce. Despite their critical contributions, many freelancers remain invisible in official credits due to subcontracting hierarchies, staff turnover, and limited credit slots. This paper proposes \textbf{AnimatorSBT}, a blockchain-based contribution certification framework that leverages Soulbound Tokens (SBTs)---non-transferable NFTs introduced by Buterin et al.~(2022)---to create immutable, verifiable records of animators' work contributions at the granular level of individual production tasks. We integrate AnimatorSBT with an existing production management tool for freelance animators and implement a prototype on the Polygon blockchain. Through cost analysis and expert evaluation, we demonstrate that the system is economically viable (minting cost $<$\$0.01 per certificate) and addresses a genuine need in the industry. Our framework contributes to the emerging literature on decentralized identity for creative gig workers and offers a replicable model for other project-based creative industries.
