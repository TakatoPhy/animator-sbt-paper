% Abstract

Japan's anime production market reached a record \textyen 362.1 billion (\$2.41 billion) in 2024, yet the freelance animators who constitute 47--70\% of the production workforce remain largely invisible in official credits due to subcontracting hierarchies, staff turnover, and limited credit slots. This paper proposes \textbf{AnimatorSBT}, a blockchain-based contribution certification framework that leverages Soulbound Tokens (SBTs)---non-transferable tokens introduced by Weyl et al.~(2022)---to create immutable, verifiable records of animators' work contributions at the granular level of individual production tasks. The framework is designed for integration with an existing production management tool for freelance animators, targeting the Polygon PoS blockchain for deployment. Through cost simulation and requirements analysis, we show that the system is economically viable (estimated minting cost $<$\$0.01 per certificate) and satisfies five requirements for an ideal credentialing solution: completeness, verifiability, persistence, portability, and minimal friction. Our framework contributes to the emerging literature on decentralized identity for creative gig workers and offers a replicable model for other project-based creative industries.
